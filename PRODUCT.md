# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers assessing Chelsea Zhao for AI education, AI application, consumer product, and gamified learning product roles. They need to understand her positioning quickly, inspect credible product evidence, and reach a resume, live product, or contact path without friction.

## Product Purpose

This bilingual portfolio turns Chelsea's teaching, statistics, internet-team, user-testing, and zero-to-one building experience into a coherent product story. Success means a visitor remembers how she works, can verify that story through Rescue Ducks and Agora, and can confidently continue to her resume or contact details.

## Positioning

Chelsea finds friction in real learning situations, turns it into a testable AI-enabled product, and uses learner feedback and evidence to decide what to improve next.

## Operating Context

The site is usually reviewed quickly during recruiting, often on a work laptop or phone and sometimes revisited directly at a project case. Visitors may be in a quiet office, so all sound is opt-in and primary content must remain usable without animation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Chinese and English routes share the same product truth and preserve page context when switching languages.
- Core proof includes the live Rescue Ducks product, the Agora case, user testing, teaching experience, data analysis, internet collaboration, education, personal interests, resume download, and email contact.
- No login, database, CMS, public phone number, fabricated user outcomes, or unverified performance claims.
- The intro plays fully once per browser, can always be skipped and replayed, defaults to muted, and degrades immediately to the page if assets or motion are unavailable.
- The site remains responsive, keyboard accessible, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

Chelsea's real identity and portrait remain intact. The established blue-sky, lake, fresh green, and warm-white world stays recognizable. The voice is thoughtful, evidence-led, warm, and specific rather than self-promotional. Rescue Ducks remains the first and strongest case.

## Evidence on Hand

- `public/hero-chelsea.png`: approved lakeside portrait composition.
- `public/rescue-ducks-live.png` and `public/agora-live.png`: real product interfaces.
- `public/Chelsea_Zhao_Product_Manager_Resume_CN.pdf`: current resume.
- Rescue Ducks: 30–40 student testers, 55 learning levels, 376 semantic groups, and access code `rd-red-4q9v`.
- Agora: implemented six-level hint mechanism, four error categories, and three evaluation dimensions; no learner-impact claim.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with a product mechanism, not a biography.
2. Let real interfaces and evidence carry claims.
3. Make creativity support comprehension.
4. Reward exploration without hiding required information.
5. Preserve a fast, quiet path for recruiting review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All essential content and interactions must work without sound, animation, hover, fine pointer control, or color-only meaning. Text remains HTML rather than being embedded into explanatory imagery when it carries essential meaning.
