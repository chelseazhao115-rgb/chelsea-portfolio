## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitors are recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Chelsea Zhao for AI education, AI application, consumer product, and gamified learning product roles. They usually scan quickly first, then selectively inspect projects and experience evidence.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio helps visitors understand Chelsea's product judgment, AI prototyping ability, education-domain insight, data literacy, and personal perspective. Success means a visitor can identify role fit and core evidence within 20–30 seconds, then explore deeper proof without reading a resume-shaped page.

## Positioning

The site turns one candidate's real path across teaching, data, internet work, and AI-built products into an interactive evidence map. Interaction exposes context and proof; it never hides essential qualifications or substitutes spectacle for substance.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from a resume link or QR code, often on desktop during candidate review and sometimes on mobile. They need bilingual navigation, downloadable resume access, project links, clear role dates, and progressively disclosed detail.

## Product Principles

1. Essential fit is visible before interaction; interaction reveals evidence.
2. Real work and verified facts outrank decorative completeness.
3. One signature interaction leads; supporting motion stays purposeful.
4. Personal character supports the professional story without becoming a lifestyle gallery.
5. Every experience remains understandable on keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings and controls, visible keyboard focus, accessible dialogs, meaningful alternative text, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly targets, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
